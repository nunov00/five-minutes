# Ranked lesson pipeline

Owner-approved direction: 18 October 2026. Activation requires the implementation PR's qualifying different-provider review and exact-head checks. This extends ADR-0012; it replaces backlog-first selection, not its identity, date, review or publication safeguards.

## Pool and shortlist

`editorial/backlog.json` retains the full editorial pool: existing revisions, original-catalogue transfers, new ideas, parked work and historical dispositions. The optional `pipeline` field is the reviewed working shortlist. Production uses it; its absence is supported only for historical snapshots/rollback. `pipeline.ranking` lists 10–20 queued entry keys, in order, each with a proposed headline, short description, reader hook, ranking reason, relevance note and zero to five evidence URLs. Start with ten. `assessedAt` is the actual RFC 3339 assessment instant with an explicit timezone (Z or offset), recorded after the current Zurich run day starts and on the same Zurich day as the candidate release timestamp (`revisedAt ?? publishedAt`) and `summary` explains important changes; do not claim measured interest or a source audit without evidence. A temporary shortfall needs an explicit reason rather than filler. Neither shortfall nor disagreement over adjacent good ideas alone blocks an otherwise strong lesson.

Shortlist membership is not a promise of publication. Retain every older entry when it falls out of the top ten. No scheduled refresh may delete, park, retire, cover, accept or rewrite another pool record or its revision criteria. Normal reviewed Manager administration handles genuine duplicates, retirement, Owner returns and blocked work. The existing return command preserves identity and records the Owner's reason; priority 0 remains historical metadata, not a permanent right to outrank better ideas. Assess a returned lesson in the same reviewed administration PR and explain its shortlist position or deferral. An explicit urgent Owner instruction takes precedence. A factual error in a published lesson needs prompt correction through the normal correction path, regardless of rank; do not wait for the daily queue.

New ideas use `kind: new`, a stable pool key and proposed slug checked against every registry route (including aliases), original/destination pool slug and legacy alias, but no fabricated lesson identity or import provenance. Daily additions use priority at least 1 (0 is reserved for Owner returns) and an actual RFC 3339 queuedAt on the run day, never backdated or in the future. Assign their real UUID and creation/publication dates only when selected. A completed new idea becomes a revision of its published destination if returned. Check the full published catalogue, historical pool and open PRs for conceptual overlap, not merely matching titles or slugs. Transfers still require substantive comparison with existing lessons before a new identity is created.

## Recording lessons marked for review

Use the existing revision entries in `editorial/backlog.json` as the single working list of published lessons marked for review. Record the feedback source, reviewed commit when known, severity and reason in `reason`, and concrete required changes and verification limits in `criteria`. Reuse the existing entry through the Owner-return path; a completed transfer becomes a revision of its published destination. Preserve earlier outstanding criteria when adding feedback. This uses the existing format and review process; it adds no new state or publication authority.

Include these revisions in the normal pool assessment alongside transfers and new ideas. Record their shortlist position or reason for deferral in the administration PR. Content remains published while queued under ADR-0012. A linked historical issue may retain the original discussion, but close it as tracking moved only after the reviewed backlog change merges, with a link to the canonical entry; closure does not mean the lesson has been corrected. Ordinary review flags do not need individual issues. Necessary blocker/recovery issues under the task contract retain their existing role.

## Ranking judgement

Judge these in order, without pretending to have objective numerical scores:

1. Reader appeal: an honest question, relatable stakes, a surprising observation or useful everyday misunderstanding. Curiosity leads; clickbait and novelty without substance fail.
2. Learning value: a clear causal explanation or meaningful change in understanding, suitable for both editions.
3. Distinctiveness: breadth against recent releases and other leading candidates; compare central mechanisms, not just topic labels.
4. Teaching potential: a concrete example, useful prediction and/or explanatory figure, plus meaningful Adult depth.
5. Evidence and readiness: accessible authoritative sources, current scope eligibility and practical feasibility in the daily run.

Relevance is a changing input to these judgements. New published articles, genuine developments, seasonal context and verified trends can move candidates up or down. Reassess rather than preserving yesterday's order by habit. Revisions and transfers receive neither automatic preference nor a penalty for age. Check the whole outstanding pool for a stronger candidate; a displaced idea remains available. Use available aggregate readership/feedback as supporting evidence only, with its date, sample limitations and no invented demand estimates. Do not add analytics services, scraping jobs, paid models or another automation.

Keep the scan proportionate: review recent publication coverage, the pool and a small number of focused searches when a relevance claim or stronger candidate warrants them. Verify time-sensitive claims in current primary sources and record source URLs, dates/findings in the PR; merely seeing a headline does not verify a trend. Empty evidence URLs are valid for explicitly editorial evergreen judgements. Lack of a verified news hook is no reason to invent one. Relevance does not broaden ADR-0007's topic/source boundary: the lesson remains an eligible evergreen explanation, not current politics, medical advice, active controversies or unstable news reporting. An ineligible idea stays outside the ready shortlist with its reason recorded via normal administration.

## Daily Writer

Read this policy from trusted current main with the task contract, before candidate data. Resume today's candidate first. Otherwise select `nextBacklogEntry` from main: the first reviewed shortlist item. Never choose today's lesson using a reordered candidate blob. Check scope, recent coverage, current relevance, duplicate history and any known blocker before drafting. If a verified development makes the reviewed first item unsuitable or another much stronger, prepare a separate, normally reviewed queue-only Manager PR before creating the day's candidate. No lesson is attempted until that change merges; missing review/access is reported. This is an exceptional pre-selection change, not another routine daily PR or permission to silently skip.

In the normal initial daily commit, complete only the selected entry and refresh the remaining shortlist in the same `editorial/backlog.json` change. Exclude the selected lesson; it is complete only if that PR merges. Reassess the full remaining pool, add up to five genuinely strong new ideas if useful, and retain at least ten viable candidates where possible. Existing good ideas mean there is no daily novelty quota. Reorder shortlist references and edit their briefs/relevance explanations; do not rewrite underlying historical records or acceptance criteria. The guard enforces preservation of all pre-existing entries and only the selected completion. It permits up to five appended queued new records and a bounded shortlist, not arbitrary administration. Complete this once with the lesson, before opening the PR. After failure/closure, main retains both its prior selection and shortlist; no same-day replacement is allowed.

If no eligible queued candidate remains, do not attempt either an unqueued lesson or an empty completion. Record and report the empty/blocked pool once, prepare or resume a normally reviewed Manager queue-only replenishment PR, and resume publication only after it merges. Do not repeatedly retry a lesson with no selection.

Record on the daily PR: selected key and why it remains suitable; previous/new top three; additions, removals from the shortlist and material movements with reasons; catalogue overlap checks; source-backed relevance findings versus editorial judgement; shortfall reason if any. Include the reviewer checklist below explicitly or link to this current-main policy. The scheduled Reviewer reviews both lesson and queue; Claude/Copilot may provide advisory feedback on both under existing arrangements. No permanent Claude requirement, new schedule or extra paid agent is introduced.

During bounded later Writer revision rounds, change the queue only to resolve an actual separate Reviewer IMPORTANT finding about it. Keep the selected completion and every pre-existing main entry immutable except that completion; re-verify the whole main-to-head delta and the ordinary three-round history. A queue-only IMPORTANT fix still consumes a revision round. Do not keep resampling ideas during lesson rewrites. Mechanical technical refresh preserves the whole candidate backlog blob byte-for-byte.

## Reviewer checklist and record

In the same separate run as lesson review, inspect the exact-head queue against trusted main and this policy. Read all available feedback on the queue as well as the lesson. Do not accept the Writer's ordering or purported trend as evidence by itself.

- Confirm today's selection is the first main-branch candidate and its identity, original dates, provenance and single completion are correct. A new candidate must have no import/revision metadata. An unmerged completion must never affect main.
- Confirm every prior pool record survives unchanged except the one selected completion. No altered acceptance criteria, parked state, covered assertion, extra completion or disguised deletion. New records are at most five queued new ideas; check conceptual duplicates against the full catalogue, pool and open PRs.
- Assess each shortlisted headline, description and hook for a credible reader benefit, explanatory depth and scope eligibility. Check stale sources and claimed trends directly when material to rank. Challenge misleading popularity claims.
- Reassess the top three independently against recent coverage and strong alternatives in the full pool; state why the next candidate deserves first place. A defensible adjacent ordering difference is MINOR at most, not a forced rewrite.
- Confirm new and old ideas compete fairly; recent publications, seasonal changes and developments genuinely inform the new order where relevant. Owner-returned work stays recorded even when deferred, with a reason.
- Confirm the selected lesson is excluded from the next shortlist, at least ten viable candidates remain or a concrete shortfall is recorded, and major changes have reasons. No filler to reach ten.
- Recheck the candidate's queue history against the first submitted head: subsequent queue edits require an actual preceding IMPORTANT queue finding and a reserved round; no unsolicited refresh during a lesson-only revision. The selected completion itself never changes.

Record `PIPELINE: PASS`, `PIPELINE: REVISE` or `PIPELINE: BLOCK`, exact SHA, count, next top three, judgement on first place, conceptual-overlap checks, source-verification limitations and dispositions of external queue findings. Unsupported material premises, an ineligible leading idea, duplicate lesson proposals or lost work warrant actionable IMPORTANT findings; forbidden scope/history changes block. A documented shortfall or honest difference in editorial preference alone does not prevent Ready. Review quality never follows from the parser accepting JSON. Apply the existing same-run Ready/guarded-merge, fresh checks, source/render, bounded revisions and same-day gates to the combined PR.

## Technical and review boundaries

The stored format remains version 1 with an additive optional validated pipeline; historical fixtures without it retain legacy priority/date/key selection. Once main has a pipeline, a daily candidate cannot remove it or bypass queue completion. The guard reads all data at pinned base/head revisions and never executes candidate data. The pool is limited to 250 entries/256 KiB and the shortlist to 20; existing lesson and image limits are unchanged. This is an editorial workflow file, never a deployed public admin endpoint.

The initial code/guard/policy change is `review:mandatory` and requires a qualifying different-provider review because it changes publication safeguards. Once accepted, ADR-0007's separate-run review exception expressly includes this bounded data-only refresh alongside the daily lesson. It does not cover arbitrary queue administration or code/policy/schema/CI changes in a lesson PR. The existing task bootstraps already read current-main contracts; their prompts and schedules stay unchanged. Activation is the reviewed merge. Roll back behaviour through a reviewed change that retains all pool records and published completion history; do not erase newly queued work.
