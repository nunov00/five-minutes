22 October 2026 single-version direction: [ADR-0013](0013-single-lesson-version.md) supersedes all earlier Adult/Teen requirements. Publish and review one lesson from `lesson.yaml` and `adult.md`; retired `teen.md` sources are inert archive material. All non-edition safeguards remain.

The returning-reader Today refinement was approved during the M6.4 device review on 2026-10-04. On 2026-10-15 the Owner approved replacing Today’s duplicated first-visit choice with one selected presentation, defaulting to Adult when no valid preference exists, under #131. ## Context

M1-M5 established the production architecture and Discovery. A deliberately isolated joint design prototype then explored how those capabilities could feel as one coherent publication.

The prototype is design evidence, not production architecture and not a branch to merge wholesale. Owner device feedback established three useful product signals: both visual voices were comfortable to read, the collapsed prediction interaction was noticeable/useful, and the seasons figure earned its place. Claude/Anthropic independently reviewed prototype head `9ef92941bd9db3e81f761a1215b73c57e144b12a` and reported 0 BLOCKER / 0 IMPORTANT / 0 MINOR for the bounded prototype implementation. That review did not approve production contracts and explicitly left real Safari/bfcache, screen-reader behaviour, final fonts and production-scale performance unverified.

This ADR records the selected design direction and the subsequently explicit Owner decisions for the production contracts. Implementation remains subject to normal focused PRs and independent review.

## Direction already selected

### One publication, two visual voices

Five Minutes remains one publication with one immutable lesson identity and mandatory Adult and Teen presentations under ADR-0006.

Adult and Teen retain the explicit canonical lesson routes from ADR-0005:

- `/adult/<slug>`
- `/teen/<slug>`

An explicit lesson URL remains authoritative for the presentation being requested. Shared publication chrome may give Adult and Teen materially different typography, density and activity treatment without turning them into separate products or identities.

The editorial rule remains unchanged: neither Adult nor Teen is the editorially “complete” edition. Adult is only the product-interface default when a neutral surface has no valid saved preference.

### Recall-before-summary direction

The selected lesson ending asks the reader to retrieve or consider the central idea **after the explanatory body and before the final takeaway/answer**. This is the intended meaning of “recall-first”; it does not move a quiz ahead of the lesson body.

The 23 October 2026 opt-in `opening: answer-first` amendment in [ADR-0006](0006-lesson-schema-and-identity.md) permits the existing takeaway once after the opening lead and before the detailed explanation. Recall still follows the body and reveals only the answer, testing a causal step beyond the opening wording. Absence of this field preserves the order above; this is not a global default change.

Essential reading, the prompt, takeaway/reveal and navigation must remain understandable without JavaScript.

### Figures and lightweight activities

The Owner approved production support for optional presentation-specific figures and optional Teen-only lightweight activities. ADR-0006 now defines schema version 2 and the safe content boundary: app-rendered diagrams consume validated structured data; raster assets are local PNG/WebP; lesson-authored SVG/HTML/executable embeds are prohibited. EDITORIAL.md owns the associated factual/accessibility review criteria.

## Owner-approved production contracts

### D1 — Neutral entry points and first visit

Today `/` shows one presentation throughout the surface. With a valid saved preference it shows that edition; otherwise it defaults to Adult without silently writing a preference. The page does not redirect. The selected edition applies to the Today card, Earlier links and Surprise Me, and the clearly labelled global edition selector remains visible. Changing the selector writes the preference and switches Today in place when JavaScript is available. Without JavaScript or with blocked/unreadable storage, Today shows Adult and the selector’s links remain usable fallbacks to the corresponding canonical lesson routes.

Library and Topics have edition-specific URLs generated by the shared URL generator: `/editions/adult/library`, `/editions/teen/library` and `/editions/<edition>/topics/<topic>`. The `/adult/<slug>` and `/teen/<slug>` namespaces remain lesson-only.

The complete edition-specific discovery route family is generated centrally:

- `/editions/<edition>/library` and `/editions/<edition>/library/page/<n>`;
- `/editions/<edition>/topics`, `/editions/<edition>/topics/<topic>` and any later accepted subtopic route beneath that same edition namespace;
- Search remains the single `/search` URL with an edition query context and is not duplicated under `/editions`.

Existing neutral discovery routes remain valid static entry pages: `/library`, `/library/page/<n>`, `/topics` and `/topics/<topic>`. With a saved preference, client enhancement forwards to the exact corresponding edition-specific route, preserving pagination/path meaning, query and hash, using `location.replace()` (or an equivalent navigation that replaces the current history entry) so the destination actually loads and Back does not bounce forward again. With no saved preference, or without JavaScript/storage, the neutral page renders a simple usable Adult/Teen chooser with links to the corresponding edition-specific destination; it never defaults to Adult. Neutral pages are indexable only according to the SEO policy chosen for chooser/forwarding surfaces; the sitemap lists canonical edition-specific discovery URLs and does not treat neutral forwarding/chooser pages as canonical edition content.

Today `/` is deliberately different: it never auto-forwards from saved preference. It presents the saved edition, or Adult when no valid preference exists, at the same URL. Choosing its global selector writes preference. Search uses that same sole global selector, restores saved preference and otherwise defaults its interface to Adult without writing the fallback.

### D2 — Preference-writing actions

Only global Adult/Teen selection surfaces write edition preference: the shared header selector and edition choices on neutral Library/Topics choosers. On Today the header selector switches the whole surface in place. On a lesson page it opens the same immutable lesson in the selected edition and shows the edition of the page currently open.

Explicit/shared lesson links never write preference. Lesson pages show the date without a redundant edition label and do not duplicate the global selector with a “Read the other version” link. If browser storage is blocked/unavailable, the selector still navigates and a quiet note explains that the choice will not be remembered.

Saved state records the immutable lesson ID together with the exact saved edition. Adult and Teen presentations can therefore be saved independently, while Read state remains shared by lesson identity. Saved and Recent lists use the exact presentation title without repeating edition labels already represented by that title and context.

### D3 — History, Return and restoration

Edition switches add normal history entries except on Today, where the selector changes the presentation in place without navigation. The initial choice on a neutral Library/Topics chooser instead completes the neutral entry handoff with replacement history, like automatic forwarding, so Back does not return to a chooser that immediately forwards again. Without JavaScript these links navigate normally and no preference forwarding occurs. Return is a normal link, never an invocation of browser Back: it targets the last Library, Topics or Search page visited in the current browsing session, otherwise Today. This origin must be represented safely as session navigation state and validated as an internal supported discovery destination before rendering a Return link.

A URL naming an edition always renders that edition. Neutral pages re-check saved preference when restored from browser cache. Safari/WebKit verification must assert these behaviours, including storage-blocked and installed-app/cold-entry fallbacks.

### D4 — Figure/activity canonical contract

ADR-0006 is amended and schema version 2 is the target content contract. Figures are optional and presentation-specific, so Adult and Teen may differ. Diagrams are rendered by application-owned code from validated structured lesson data. Lesson content accepts no SVG or HTML. Raster images are repository-local PNG/WebP only, require alt text and caption, and are subject to configured size and deployment file-count limits.

Activities are optional and Teen-only. EDITORIAL.md defines review criteria for factual accuracy, captions/alt text and activity answers/explanations.

## Discovery and catalogue semantics

M5 remains authoritative. Static Library/Topics, compact catalogue, edition-specific lazy search, deterministic Related and Surprise Me are preserved.

“Bounded” refers to **rendered/page presentation and payload purpose**, not a prohibition on loading the compact catalogue. ADR-0003 intentionally allows consumers such as Saved and Surprise Me to load the compact catalogue. Search indexes remain lazy and edition-specific.

## Delivery sequence

This ADR uses the same M6.1-M6.4 sequence as OPERATIONS.md and roadmap #4; it does not define a parallel staging model:

1. **M6.1 production-experience foundation** — shared shell/navigation; complete ADR-0005 neutral/edition discovery routing; D2-D3 preference/history/Return behaviour; and the final versioned reader-state envelope/storage abstraction **before the first production preference write**. No temporary edition-only key.
2. **M6.2 reading/content enhancements** — schemaVersion 2 validation/rendering for ADR-0006 figures and Teen activities plus recall-before-takeaway.
3. **M6.3 reader-state surfaces** — Saved/Read/recent/appearance and export/import using the same M6.1 envelope; migrate only genuine pre-M6 legacy local state where needed.
4. **M6.4 integration gate** — verify the complete production experience and reader state, including neutral forwarding/chooser behaviour, no-JS/storage-blocked cases, Return/session origin, Safari/WebKit Back/bfcache and installed-app navigation.

M7 remains Automated publication and must understand schemaVersion 2 before automated workers may author figure/activity fields. M8 remains the broader production-hardening gate and is not replaced by M6.4.

## Prototype boundary and verification

The `/design-preview` branch remains reference material only. Reusing an idea or small pattern requires production assessment; no prototype bundling, fixture, route or test result becomes a production dependency by implication.

Production adoption must verify the relevant accepted contracts, including:

- real Safari/WebKit Back/forward-cache and form-restoration behaviour;
- installed-app cold launch/navigation and PWA start semantics;
- screen-reader semantics, focus order, keyboard behaviour and enlarged-text layouts;
- narrow mobile layouts and final production fonts/tokens;
- no-JavaScript essential reading/reveal/navigation;
- realistic catalogue/search performance and Cloudflare file/asset capacity;
- safe-content/security-policy validation for any new figure/activity representation;
- editorial/factual review of figures, captions, activity prompts and answers;
- existing URL/static/redirect integrity and progressive-enhancement gates.

## Consequences

- M1-M5 remain authoritative and are extended by the approved ADR-0005/0006/0009 contracts.
- The selected visual/readability direction and D1-D4 production contracts are Owner-approved; prototype implementation details outside them cannot be inferred as production requirements.
- Existing explicit Adult/Teen lesson URLs and immutable identity are preserved.
- M6 storage is designed once before the first production preference write.
- Figures/activities cannot bypass ADR-0006 or schema versioning.
- M8 remains the final production-hardening milestone rather than being duplicated here.
